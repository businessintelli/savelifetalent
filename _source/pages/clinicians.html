Common questions

How much experience do I need?

Most roles ask for at least one year in the specialty. Some per diem and long-term care roles accept new graduates. Your recruiter will tell you what fits.

Do you help with travel housing?

Yes. Your recruiter covers housing options and stipends for travel assignments.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. We are paid by the facility.

How is my data used for matching?

Only your professional details (license, specialty, experience, preferences) are used to suggest roles. See our privacy policy.
