Questions facilities ask

How fast can you fill an urgent shift?

Call the 24/7 desk. For same-day needs we confirm availability by phone while matching runs, and send profiles as soon as a qualified clinician accepts.

Are clinicians your employees?

Yes. Clinicians are W-2 employees of Save Life Talent, so we carry payroll, taxes, insurance and workers' compensation.

Do you require a contract or minimums?

No long-term commitment. We agree on bill rates and terms up front and you staff as needed.

Does AI decide who works on my unit?

No. Matching produces a ranked shortlist. A recruiter reviews it, and you approve every clinician.

Tell us what your unit needs.

Share the role, shift and start date. We'll confirm availability.
