Information we collect

When you contact us, request staff or apply for work, we collect the details you provide: name, contact information, facility details, professional licenses, certifications, work history and any documents you upload.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to requests, match clinicians to assignments, verify credentials, process payroll and meet legal obligations.

AI-assisted matching

Our matching and credential tools use professional qualifications and stated preferences only. They do not use age, race, gender, religion, disability or other protected characteristics. A recruiter reviews every match. We do not sell your data or use it to train public AI models.

Sharing

We share clinician profiles with facilities only for assignments you are being considered for, and with service providers (background checks, payroll) under confidentiality agreements.
